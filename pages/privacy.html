Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Nokva Marketplace, we value your privacy. We collect very limited personal information to help facilitate your culinary orders, specifically:

  • Personal Details: Your full name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses when voluntarily provided.
  • Local Storage Data: We store login state, user details, bookmarked items, and developer application registrations client-side inside your browser's local storage to provide a seamless application experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide, maintain, and optimize Nokva Marketplace features, including:

  • Pre-filling order and support forms for convenience.
  • Maintaining lists of bookmarked delicacies.
  • Handling platform support inquiries.

3. Data Protection

All data persists locally in your client's web browser storage (localStorage). Since we do not transmit your personal profile information to external central databases, you maintain complete and ultimate control over your data. You can completely erase your profile at any time by selecting "Delete Account" inside your Account Settings page or clearing your browser cookies and site data.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Customer Support Hub or email us at support@nokvamarketplace.com.
