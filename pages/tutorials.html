App Tutorials & Guides

Learn how to browse fresh food, place orders, save favorites to your bookmarks, and register developer applications.

Gourmet Shop & Placing Orders

1 Browse the Shop Menu

On the homepage, you will find a curated selection of fresh dishes, ranging from artisan burgers to classic pizzas and fresh beverages. Each item card lists a description, tags, and pricing details.

2 Login or Register to Place Orders

If you click "Order Now" while signed out, you will be prompted to create an account first. Sign up or log in to access the secure client-side order form.

3 Complete Your Order

Once logged in, click "Order Now" on your preferred dish. The page will automatically pre-select your item. Fill in your quantity, choose between pickup or delivery, review your address, and hit "Confirm Order".

Bookmarks & Account Settings

1 Bookmarking Items

Logged-in users can click the ribbon/bookmark icon located in the upper-right corner of each food card. Bookmarked items will sync automatically with your local storage session.

2 View Bookmarked Items

Access your curated list at any time by clicking "Bookmarks" in the top header navigation or in the website footer. You can easily click "Order Now" on your bookmarked items, or click the bookmark ribbon again to remove them.

3 Personalize Account Settings

Go to the "Settings" page in the header menu to update your profile metadata, phone number, default address, and newsletter preferences. You can also completely delete your client-side profile from settings.

Developer Hub Integration

1 Visit the Developer Hub

Nokva Marketplace provides open culinary APIs. Locate the "Developers" link in the footer or the header actions to navigate to our interactive Developer Hub.

2 Register a Developer Application

Provide an application name, description, and redirect URI callback. Upon registration, Nokva generates a secure, randomized Client ID and Client Secret for authorization flows.

3 Revoke Credentials

If your application credentials are compromised, or if you no longer require integration, click the "Revoke" button on your application card in the Developer Hub dashboard to permanently invalidate them.

Creating Developer Apps Tutorials

1 Access the Developer Hub

Go to the Developers page from the footer link to register and manage your custom applications.

2 Register a New App

Provide an application name, description, and redirect URI, then click Register Application to generate credentials.

3 Secure and Revoke Credentials

You can securely copy your Client ID and Client Secret, or permanently revoke them if your application changes.
